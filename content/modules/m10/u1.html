Why this matters

Scenario opener

Remote work moves business activity into homes, cafes, trains, hotel rooms, and personal devices. The workplace no longer has walls, so the security boundary follows the worker instead.

Core concept

Remote work expands the attack surface to include home networks, household dynamics, personal-device overlap, and public physical spaces.

This does not mean remote work is unsafe by default. It means more of the security environment is now shaped by the worker's choices instead of office infrastructure.

The main remote-work risk areas

  • Home networks and routers.
  • Family members, visitors, and shared physical space.
  • Blur between personal and work devices or accounts.
  • Travel, public spaces, and attended-device discipline.

Exercise

Takeaway

Remote work moves the perimeter to wherever you work. The useful question is not "am I remote," but "what does this place add to the risk surface?"
