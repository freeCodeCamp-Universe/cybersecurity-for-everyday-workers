Why this matters

Scenario opener

Public spaces do not only create network risk. They create visibility risk, audio leakage, and the possibility of losing the device itself in a moment of distraction.

Core concept

Public spaces add visibility, audio leakage, and attended-device risk. Some work is fine there; some work should wait for a private setting.

The key skill is matching the task to the space instead of assuming comfort means appropriateness.

The public-space decision rule

  • Routine email and writing are often fine with a privacy-aware setup.
  • Sensitive calls, payroll, and confidential project work usually belong in private spaces.
  • Never leave the device unattended, even briefly.
  • Use privacy filters, good seating position, and mobile data when needed.

Exercise

Takeaway

Match the work to the space. Some tasks belong in private settings regardless of how normal public work has started to feel.
