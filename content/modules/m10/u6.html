Why this matters

Scenario opener

Travel concentrates remote-work risk into a short period: new networks, public visibility, border concerns, tired decisions, and more physical exposure of the device. Preparation before departure does more than improvisation on the road.

Core concept

Travel concentrates remote-work risks, which is why preparation before travel is more effective than trying to invent safe habits while already in transit.

The device, the network, the physical environment, and even legal context may all change during travel. A simple plan reduces how much you have to decide under pressure.

The three travel phases

Pre-departure

Update devices, confirm MFA, reduce unnecessary data, and save key contacts off-device.

In transit

Protect visibility, keep the device with you, and avoid doing highly sensitive work in exposed spaces.

At destination

Use hotel-safe or secure storage, follow network rules, and know what to do if the device is lost.

What to do

Build the trip before the trip starts

If your role has border-crossing concerns, sensitive client material, or special employer requirements, those decisions should be settled before departure. Travel is not the time to discover policy gaps.

Exercise

Takeaway

Travel security is mostly preparation. A short pre-trip routine prevents many of the in-trip problems that feel hardest to solve once you are already away.
