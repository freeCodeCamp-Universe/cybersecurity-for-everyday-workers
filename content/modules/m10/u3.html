Why this matters

Scenario opener

Video-call leaks usually come from things people forget are visible or audible: notifications, background paperwork, a whole-screen share, or a family conversation in the next room.

Core concept

Video calls leak information through background visibility, room audio, and screen-sharing choices. Each leak path is preventable with a short routine.

You do not need a perfect studio setup. You need a repeatable pre-call habit that catches the most common errors before the meeting starts.

The pre-call checklist that matters

  • Check the background or enable blur.
  • Use headphones and mute when not speaking.
  • Share a single application or window instead of the whole screen.
  • Turn on do-not-disturb and close chat or email before sharing.

Exercise

Takeaway

A short pre-call routine prevents many ordinary meeting leaks. Repeatability matters more than complexity.
