Why this matters

Scenario opener

A safe home setup is mostly a series of small physical and configuration choices. The goal is not perfection. It is making the everyday environment less likely to leak information or invite casual access.

Core concept

A small set of physical and configuration choices at home covers most realistic remote-work risk: screen orientation, device storage, network separation, automatic locking, and a deliberate end-of-day habit.

These controls matter because home is a real workplace, even if it does not feel formal. What would be obvious in an office policy still matters in a living room or kitchen.

Your home-work baseline

  • Face screens away from windows, doors, and shared space.
  • Use a privacy filter where shoulder-surfing is likely.
  • Keep a lockable place for the device when away for long periods.
  • Separate work devices from guest or smart-home networks when possible.
  • Use short auto-lock and a clear end-of-day routine.

Exercise

Takeaway

Five small setup choices cover most home-work risk. None are dramatic, and that is exactly why they work well as habits.
