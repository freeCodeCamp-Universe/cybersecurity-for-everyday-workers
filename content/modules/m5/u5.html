Why this matters

Scenario opener

Your home router is easy to ignore because it quietly works in the background. But it is the gateway every work device in your house depends on, and factory-default convenience is not the same thing as a safe setup.

Core concept

Your home router is the gateway between your work and the open internet. Default settings are designed for easy installation, not strong security.

This does not mean you need enterprise networking skills. A small number of baseline settings handle most realistic home-network risk: strong admin access, strong Wi-Fi settings, updates, and separation between work devices and everything else.

The five baseline settings

  • Change the default router admin password.
  • Use WPA3 if supported, or WPA2 with a long unique passphrase.
  • Keep firmware updated.
  • Use a guest network for visitors and smart-home devices when possible.
  • Disable remote management and UPnP unless there is a specific reason to keep them on.
Why separation matters

Your smart bulb is not a work device

A poorly maintained TV, camera, toy, or smart bulb does not need to cause a dramatic attack to matter. If it sits on the same network as your work laptop, it increases the number of weak points inside the home environment. Separation reduces that risk path.

Takeaway

Five settings on a home router handle most realistic risk. If you never changed the defaults, assume there is easy value here.
