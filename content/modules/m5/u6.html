Why this matters

Scenario opener

Travel mixes public networks with fatigue, distraction, and unfamiliar environments. Even good rules feel harder to apply when you are rushing through an airport or checking into a hotel late at night.

Core concept

Travel networks combine public-Wi-Fi risk with an environment where you are tired, time-pressured, and less likely to verify carefully.

That is why the best travel-network policy is decided before the trip. A prepared rule beats an improvised decision made in a hurry.

Your travel default

  • Use mobile data for the most sensitive tasks when possible.
  • Use Wi-Fi for routine browsing only after confirming you are on the right network and HTTPS is in use.
  • Turn on the work VPN whenever your employer requires it for access or policy.
  • Update devices and confirm MFA before leaving home so you are not doing setup on the road.
Common mistake

Tired and rushed is part of the threat model

The technical environment matters, but so does your state of mind. Attackers do not need a perfect exploit if they can rely on confusion, low battery, and impatience making you skip the normal checks.

Exercise

Takeaway

Decide your travel rules before you travel. The worst time to invent a network policy is when you are tired, rushed, and already connected to the wrong thing.
