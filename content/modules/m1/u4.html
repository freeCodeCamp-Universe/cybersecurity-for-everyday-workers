Why this matters

Scenario opener

Rina's password for a supplier portal is stolen in a phishing attack. The attacker tries to log in that night. Because the account also requires a second factor that Rina controls, the stolen password is not enough on its own.

Core concept

Multi-factor authentication adds a second lock. It requires proof from more than one category, so a password alone does not grant access.

This does not make every login perfect, but it changes the economics of the attack. Many common compromises depend on stolen passwords being usable immediately. MFA breaks that assumption.

What counts as a different factor

Something you know

Password, PIN, or passphrase. Two different passwords do not count as two factors.

Something you have

A phone, authenticator app, or hardware security key that the attacker does not physically control.

Something you are

Biometrics such as fingerprint or face unlock, usually backed by a device PIN.

Strength order

How the common MFA options compare

SMS codes are widely available and still much better than no MFA, but they depend on the phone number system. Authenticator apps generate time-based codes on your device and avoid that weakness. Hardware security keys offer the strongest protection because they are harder to phish and are tied more tightly to the real site.

The practical rule is simple: use the strongest option the system actually supports, but do not delay enabling MFA while waiting for the perfect method.

Common mistake

Two real-world misunderstandings

  • "SMS is weak, so I will wait until TOTP is offered." This leaves the account with no second factor at all.
  • "I can leave the hardware key plugged into the shared workstation." Once it is left with the device, it no longer behaves like a separate factor.
What to do

Where to enable MFA first

  1. 1
    Enable MFA on work email first, because email often controls other account resets.
  2. 2
    Next secure finance, payroll, HR, admin, and VPN accounts.
  3. 3
    Store backup codes or recovery methods somewhere safe so you are not locked out later.

Exercise

Takeaway

MFA is one of the highest-impact upgrades you can make quickly. Even the weakest MFA is a major improvement over password-only login, and stronger options reduce risk further.
