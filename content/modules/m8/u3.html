Why this matters

Scenario opener

The worst time to discover the reporting path is while your account may already be compromised. Incident channels should be known before the stressful moment, not searched for during it.

Core concept

Every organization has a reporting path. The problem is usually not that it does not exist, but that people do not know it until they need it.

A good report route depends on the type of incident and whether your normal device or account can still be trusted. That is why finding the paths in advance is itself part of readiness.

What your first report should include

  • What you saw.
  • When it happened.
  • What you did so far.
  • What you have not done yet.
What to do

Save the urgent channels off-device

If your phone or laptop is the thing that may be compromised or missing, the reporting path needs to exist somewhere else too. A card, notebook, or another trusted device is enough.

Exercise

Takeaway

Find the reporting paths before you need them. Preparation removes hesitation when speed matters most.
