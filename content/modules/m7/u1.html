Why this matters

Scenario opener

When everything is treated as equally sensitive, work slows down and people bypass the rules. When nothing is treated as special, real harm gets exposed. Classification exists to avoid both failures.

Core concept

Different types of data carry different consequences if they leak. Classification matches the level of protection to the level of consequence.

The point is not secrecy for its own sake. The point is making sure the handling effort matches the damage a leak would cause to people, customers, the organization, or legal obligations.

What classification helps you decide

  • Where the data can be stored.
  • Who can access it and how it can be shared.
  • How long it should be kept.
  • What level of review or logging is appropriate.
Important idea

Classification is about consequence, not drama

A published blog post may be harmless if public, but highly restricted if it includes salary data in hidden comments. A name alone may be low sensitivity, but a name plus diagnosis or account details can move the same artifact to a much higher level.

Exercise

Takeaway

Classification matches protection to consequence. Without it, organizations become either too slow or too exposed.
