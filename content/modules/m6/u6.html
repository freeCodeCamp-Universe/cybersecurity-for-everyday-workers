Why this matters

Scenario opener

A vague access request forces someone else to guess what you need. A clean request reduces friction, speeds approval, and makes the eventual revoke easier too.

Core concept

A good access request is faster to approve and easier to revoke because it gives the approver the information they need without forcing them to interpret your intent.

The structure of the ask shapes the structure of the grant. If the request is broad and vague, the answer will either be slow, denied, or broader than necessary.

The four questions every good request answers

  1. 1
    What specific system, role, or scope do you need?
  2. 2
    Why does the work require it?
  3. 3
    How long should the access last before review or end?
  4. 4
    What alternative did you consider before asking?
What to do

Write for auditability

Keep the request in writing. A written request protects you as well as the organization because it records what was asked, why it was needed, and what was approved.

Exercise

Takeaway

A clean request gets a faster yes, a more useful no, and a smaller grant that is easier to remove later.
