Why this matters

Scenario opener

A lost laptop is easiest to protect in the first hour, while it still has power and a network connection and before anyone else has had time to use it. Delay turns a recovery problem into a breach problem.

Core concept

A lost device becomes a security incident the moment you cannot account for it. Speed of response decides whether the loss stays small.

The priority is not solving the whole incident alone. It is starting the right sequence fast enough: locate, lock, notify, and prepare to wipe or change access if needed.

The first-hour sequence

  1. 1
    Try to locate the device using the recovery service enabled on it.
  2. 2
    Remotely lock it if the platform allows.
  3. 3
    Notify IT or the security contact immediately, even if you still think it may be nearby.
  4. 4
    Change high-risk passwords from another trusted device, starting with email.
Important limit

Do not make the reporting decision alone

If the device held client data, regulated data, or open sessions, reporting obligations may exist beyond your personal judgment. Escalate internally first and let the organization decide the formal reporting path.

What to do now

Prepare before loss happens

  • Enable Find My or Find My Device on every work device that supports it.
  • Confirm you can sign into the recovery service from another device.
  • Store the incident contact path somewhere you can access without the missing device.

Exercise

Takeaway

Have a plan before you need one. The first hour after a device is lost is when you still have the best chance to limit what comes next.
